Cold Virus

33–35 °C   |   91–95 °F

Rhinovirus family

The rhinovirus is the most common viral infectious agent in humans. Spread worldwide, it's the predominant cause of the common cold.

Symptoms include sore throat, runny nose, congestion, sneezing, coughing; sometimes muscle aches, fatigue, or headache. Fever and exhaustion are more typical of influenza.
