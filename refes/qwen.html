Rhinovirus

33°C — 35°C

The most common viral infectious agent in humans. A master of disguise, evolving constantly to bypass our immune defenses.

Symptoms include sore throat, runny nose, nasal congestion, sneezing and coughing; sometimes accompanied by muscle aches, fatigue, malaise, headache, muscle weakness, or loss of appetite.

Fever and extreme exhaustion are more usual in influenza, distinguishing the common cold from more severe respiratory infections.
